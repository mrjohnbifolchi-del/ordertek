Federal vs Provincial Digital Forensics in Canada

The tech needs are not the same. Federal work often demands air-gapped, high-GPU systems for heavy workloads. Provincial and municipal teams frequently need mobile, cost-efficient, offline-resilient units. We design Ordertek systems in tiers to match those realities.

Key takeaway: match the workstation tier to the operational environment rather than buying a one-size-fits-all box.

Breaking Encryption Barriers in the Lab

Modern full-disk encryption (BitLocker and equivalents) changes what a forensic workstation must deliver. High core counts, strong GPUs for password recovery tools, fast storage, and reliable write-blocker integration all matter. Hope is not a strategy — proper hardware is.

Why Canadian Pricing Matters for Police Tech

Many competitors quote in USD. For Canadian departments that creates currency risk and budget friction. Ordertek prices in Canadian dollars and builds with local support in mind so procurement is simpler and more predictable.

What Goes Into a Reliable Forensic Build

ECC memory, server-grade networking options, liquid cooling for sustained loads, expansion for write blockers and multi-drive imaging, and a real multi-year hot-swap warranty. These are the details that keep a system useful for years in a busy lab.
