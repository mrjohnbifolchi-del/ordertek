About Ordertek

Welcome to Ordertek — where quality and service unite.

For over 8 years we have specialized in forensic workstations that meet the unique needs of digital forensic professionals and Canadian law enforcement.

We believe acquiring a high-quality forensic workstation should be straightforward. Our team works directly with examiners to design solutions from the ground up, tailored to specific requirements and budget.

We partner with leading suppliers for top brands and current technology. Every system is rigorously tested before delivery. Our network also lets us source difficult-to-find and end-of-life components, making us a practical one-stop resource for forensic IT needs.

What sets us apart

  • Canadian pricing (CDN) — no USD conversion surprises
  • Direct collaboration with forensic examiners
  • Custom builds including high-core Threadripper PRO platforms and Supermicro options
  • 3-year hot-swap warranty
  • Focus on real-world lab and field requirements (write blockers, ECC, expansion, quiet reliable cooling)

Our services

  • Custom-built forensic workstations
  • Sourcing difficult-to-find computer parts
  • Custom Sager notebooks

Mission: Empower forensic professionals with reliable, cutting-edge tools and a seamless experience from quote to delivery.
